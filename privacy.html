Privacy Policy

At APEX Workforce Solution, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when registering on our website or contacting us. This includes:

  • For Workers: Name, phone number, address, skills, experience, and ID proofs.
  • For Clients: Name, contact details, business address, and specific manpower requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used for providing our core services. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Match the right clients with the right workers.
  • Communicate with you regarding job opportunities, requirements, or service updates.
  • Maintain our internal database for admin operations.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared between the respective client and worker to facilitate employment, exactly as per your request and requirement.

4. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to keep your data safe. Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized personnel (Admins) only.
